**SCRUM-21 — View Service Centers (User Guide)**

**Summary**
This guide helps users to find and understand the list of service centers and how to read availability information. It is for normal users who want to choose a center for booking.

**Scope**
- How to open service center list.
- How to read availability and status.
- Troubleshooting common problems.
- Screenshots placeholders are suggested.

**Open service center list — steps**

1. Go to main website and find link or menu `Service Centers` or open `/service-centers` page.
2. Page shows list of centers with name, address, and basic info.
3. Use search box or filters to find center by name, city, or services.
4. Click center name to see detail page with more information and availability.

**Interpreting availability**

- Each center shows availability indicators (example):
  - `Available` or green dot = there are free slots.
  - `Limited` or yellow dot = few slots left.
  - `Full` or red dot = no slots available now.
- Some pages show daily schedule or hourly slots. Look for times with `book` or `reserve` button.
- `Capacity` or `current queue` numbers show how busy center is now. Higher number means longer wait.
- If center shows `closed` or no times, it may be not open on that day.

**Details on center page**

- Contact information: phone, email, address — use to ask questions.
- Services: list of services offered (e.g., vaccination, permit processing). Make sure your need is listed.
- Opening hours: check to see if center open today and hours.

**Common problems and how to fix**

- Problem: "I cannot find my city or center"
  - Cause: filter or search terms not matching.
  - Fix: try different keywords, remove filters, or check broader area.

- Problem: "Center shows Full or no availability"
  - Cause: all slots are booked for chosen date.
  - Fix: try different date, sign up for waitlist (if available), or contact center.

- Problem: "Times shown but cannot book"
  - Cause: you may not be logged in or session expired.
  - Fix: login first, refresh page, or clear browser cache and try again.

- Problem: "Availability numbers look wrong"
  - Cause: temporary sync delay between system and center.
  - Fix: refresh page after some minutes or contact support.

**Tips for users**

- Use filters to narrow results (city, service type).
- Book earlier in the day for more options.
- Save favorite centers if app supports it.
